SMART CONSTRUCTION Dashboard
[Purpose]
"Visualization" of the customer's construction site in three dimensions by organically capturing everything existing at the site through the use of IOT.
[Functions]
(i)      Creation of 3D topography of the site: A function to generate and view 3D topography of the site by converting the point cloud data gathered by drones and 3D laser scanners into 3D topography data and removing obstacles (man-made objects and trees, etc.);
